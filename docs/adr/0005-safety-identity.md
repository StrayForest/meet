# ADR-0005 — Adult-network trust, identity and private-home safety
Status: ACCEPTED

18+; no dating solicitation/random DM/public star rating. Strong identity for private-home hosts. Documents stay with external verifier. Exact private location is envelope-encrypted and occurrence-scoped. Staff identity separate/MFA. Moderation supports multiple reports per case and immutable/minimized evidence snapshots. AI triages but is not sole irreversible authority.
