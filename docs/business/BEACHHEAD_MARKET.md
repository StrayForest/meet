# Beachhead market — Finland launch

## Geographic strategy
Product coverage may be nationwide, but **social activation is city-by-city**. Helsinki metropolitan area is the first liquidity proof. Nationwide imported supply is useful for SEO/discovery and future activation; it must not create the illusion of a healthy social marketplace.

Activation order is evidence-driven. Tampere, Turku, Oulu and other cities open for active growth only after Helsinki liquidity/playbook thresholds are credible.

## Initial user wedges
Meet remains an 18+ network for broad future use, but acquisition initially concentrates on cohorts with unusually high need for new social connections:
- people new to Helsinki/Finland or rebuilding a local social circle;
- international/English-speaking residents and newcomers;
- Finnish-speaking adults seeking activity-first social connection;
- Russian-speaking residents/newcomers seeking local IRL activity and social discovery.

## Launch languages — fixed
**Finnish, English and Russian (`fi/en/ru`) are all launch languages.** None is an optional later localization. They are part of the initial reachable market and early-density strategy.

The product must not isolate language communities by default. Event language, user language preferences and multilingual discovery should help users cross communities when they choose.

## What beachhead does not mean
- no promise that every Finnish municipality has equal event completeness;
- no targeting only expats or only Russian speakers as the permanent brand;
- no dating positioning;
- no broad paid acquisition before local liquidity and repeat behaviour are demonstrated.

## Expansion gate
A new city receives active GTM spend only when the Helsinki playbook has measurable answers for: supply acquisition, first-session relevance, social-density seeding, join→attendance, repeat participation, moderation/support and acquisition economics.
