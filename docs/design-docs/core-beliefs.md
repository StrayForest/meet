# Design core beliefs

1. **Show the plan, not the platform.** Time, place and action are more important than product chrome.
2. **Event first, people second.** Attendee context reduces uncertainty; it must not turn discovery into profile shopping.
3. **One decision at a time.** Each screen has one dominant CTA.
4. **Calm confidence beats excitement theater.** No excessive confetti, exclamation marks, streaks or urgency dark patterns.
5. **Safety is contextual.** Show relevant verification/address/privacy information where the user makes a risky decision.
6. **Progressive disclosure.** Advanced filters, full descriptions and policy details are available without crowding first scan.
7. **Familiar patterns win.** Native navigation, sheets, forms and back behavior over novelty.
8. **Accessibility is design quality.** Cognitive clarity, target size, contrast, screen readers and text scaling are product requirements.
9. **Real images over decorative graphics.** Authentic activity photography carries emotion; interface stays quiet.
10. **Low-density states are designed states.** Widen radius, offer imported events and creation prompts; never show an unexplained blank feed.
