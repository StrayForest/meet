# City liquidity model

A city is healthy only when users repeatedly see relevant opportunities with credible social activity. Raw event count and MAU are insufficient.

## Liquidity is multidimensional
The practical marketplace cell is not merely `city`. It is approximately:

`city × category/intent × time window × language compatibility × travel radius/area`.

Age/eligibility or other event constraints may further narrow an occurrence, but must not become unnecessary profiling dimensions. A city can have high aggregate MAU and still feel empty because demand is fragmented across cells.

**Fragmentation is a first-order marketplace risk.** Before opening more categories, time windows or cities, measure whether added breadth dilutes social density in the cells that already matter.

## Core liquidity metrics
- relevant opportunities per active user for next 7 days;
- Opportunity Success Rate: users finding >=1 opportunity seriously considered;
- socially-active opportunities per active user for next 7 days;
- empty-social-card rate where social context is relevant;
- median/p25 attendees or expressed social intent per eligible occurrence/cell;
- time to first useful event view, meaningful intent and successful IRL participation;
- decision success, join conversion and join→attendance;
- find-company availability/use and solo-to-social attendance where enabled;
- 30/60-day repeat successful IRL participation;
- supply freshness/cancellation correctness;
- organizer response/approval latency where applicable;
- concentration/fragmentation by category × time × language × radius.

## Initial Helsinki targets
Starting experiment targets, calibrated with evidence:
- >=80% of activated users see >=10 relevant upcoming physical opportunities within configured radius/7 days, while Opportunity Success Rate confirms that quantity is useful rather than noisy;
- >=60% see >=3 opportunities with meaningful Meet social activity in relevant activated cells;
- empty-social-card rate trends downward as activation grows;
- median time-to-first-meaningful-intent <7 days for activated users;
- H2–H6 from `PMF_HYPOTHESES.md` are evaluated by acquisition/language/category and relevant liquidity cell.

Ten mediocre events are not success if users seriously consider none. One highly relevant event can be more valuable than a large undifferentiated feed.

## Density before breadth
If Helsinki has weak social density, do not solve it by opening more cities or indiscriminately importing breadth. Concentrate acquisition and host/organizer seeding in a small number of categories, areas and time windows where recurring density can form.

Nationwide event coverage remains useful for discovery/SEO/future activation, but it must never be represented internally or externally as nationwide social liquidity.

## City activation state
`COVERAGE_ONLY → SEEDING → ACTIVE → HEALTHY`.

- COVERAGE_ONLY: imported/public supply exists; no promise of social density.
- SEEDING: targeted hosts/communities/acquisition; marketplace cells watched frequently.
- ACTIVE: enough relevant/social supply for controlled public growth.
- HEALTHY: repeat behaviour and endogenous/referral activity meet sustained thresholds across the priority cells, without excessive founder/manual orchestration.

Status is internal product/ops truth and must not be inferred from population, aggregate event count or MAU alone.
