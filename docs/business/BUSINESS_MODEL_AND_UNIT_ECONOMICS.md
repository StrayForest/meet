# Business model and unit economics

## Monetization order
1. free consumer participation and supply acquisition;
2. promoted events with relevance/safety constraints;
3. Org Pro tooling/analytics/communication value;
4. affiliate/external ticket economics where terms allow;
5. native ticketing/payments only after PMF and separate legal/payment review;
6. optional consumer premium only if it does not paywall core social participation.

## Bottom-up model
Model economics by active city, not global TAM headlines. Finland is the PMF laboratory and first operating market; venture-scale value requires a repeatable city/country expansion engine rather than assuming Finland alone supports the end-state valuation.

For each city track assumptions/ranges for:
- active users and successful/repeat IRL participants/month;
- verified/active organizations;
- organizer paid conversion and ARPA;
- promoted-event buyers, campaigns and average spend;
- affiliate-qualified transactions and net revenue;
- payment/provider fees;
- support/moderation/community operations;
- cloud/data/provider costs;
- paid acquisition and partnership costs;
- founder/community seeding hours until they become negligible or delegable.

## Required unit economics
- CAC per activated user;
- CAC per first successful IRL participant;
- **CAC per repeat successful IRL participant** — preferred mature consumer acquisition metric;
- consumer LTV where directly monetized;
- organization CAC, ARPA, gross retention and LTV;
- gross margin and contribution margin by revenue stream;
- payback period;
- revenue and contribution margin per active city;
- infra/provider cost per successful IRL participation;
- community/ops cost per successful IRL participation.

## Organizer value thesis
The strongest B2B proposition is not merely `publish an event`; it is measurable access to relevant local people who actually participate. Test willingness to pay for outcomes such as qualified reach, promotion, repeat audiences, attendee/engagement analytics, check-in/workflow and community communication.

Do not build a large B2B suite before interviews/pilots show which outcomes organizations value.

## Pre-PMF rule
Do not optimize gross revenue before proving repeat offline value. Early organizer willingness-to-pay interviews/pilots are encouraged because they test B2B value without requiring huge MAU.

## Pricing experiments
Pricing is hypothesis-driven. Record offer, target organizer, price, acceptance/rejection reason, realized value and renewal intent. Never invent advanced B2B features solely to justify a subscription tier.

## Revenue guardrail
Monetization must not corrupt discovery. Promoted inventory remains relevant/labeled and cannot displace safety/trust truth. Core social participation stays accessible enough to preserve network effects.
