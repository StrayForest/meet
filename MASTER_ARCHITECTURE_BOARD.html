Non-negotiable invariants

External ticket is admission, never social join mode.
EventOccurrence is the unit for participation, check-in, Pods and private location.
Dedupe merge preserves aliases, deep links and provenance.
Native recurrence is intentionally limited and timezone/DST tested.
Private exact address is never in generic Event/feed/map payload.
Operational safety flags do not depend on PostHog.
Push/WebSocket are not source of truth; REST/database state wins.
Staff identity is separate from consumer User identity.
