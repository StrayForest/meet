# Investor and company milestones

Milestones are evidence gates, not vanity-number targets. Architecture quality reduces execution risk; it is not itself proof of enterprise value.

## Gate 0 — build/learn readiness
- Phase 0 repository/runtime foundation works from fresh checkout;
- Helsinki supply connectors/licensing path proven for launch categories;
- fi/en/ru end-to-end localization baseline exists;
- analytics can attribute acquisition → intent → attendance/repeat;
- weekly customer-discovery cadence is running;
- product/tech work does not consume the entire founder operating week; organizer/community discovery is active.

## Gate 1 — problem/activation evidence
- Helsinki relevance/liquidity H1 credible in selected cohorts/categories;
- first 500–1,000 real users acquired through identifiable channels;
- meaningful H2 intent conversion;
- qualitative evidence explains both activation and failure modes;
- priority opportunities are socially non-empty often enough to test the social thesis.

## Gate 2 — offline value evidence
- join→attendance H3 credible;
- 30-day repeat H4 shows a stable promising cohort;
- selected events/categories have non-empty social activity with declining founder/manual seeding;
- severe safety incidents and moderation load remain operationally manageable;
- social mechanics demonstrate incremental value beyond passive event discovery.

## Gate 3 — distribution/network evidence
- at least one scalable or compounding acquisition channel shows credible CAC-to-repeat-successful-participation economics;
- SEO/public web, referrals, partnerships or another channel shows evidence of compounding rather than only paid traffic;
- referral/organic share grows;
- city liquidity can be reproduced across additional categories/areas;
- organizers actively use/ask for audience/community tooling;
- network-effect metrics show existing users/social proof improve future participation.

## Gate 4 — monetization evidence
- paid organizer/promotion pilots demonstrate willingness to pay and renewal intent;
- contribution economics are modeled with real provider/community/moderation costs;
- revenue growth does not degrade consumer trust/relevance;
- organizer value is tied to qualified reach/attendance/relationship outcomes rather than listing access alone.

## Gate 5 — expansion readiness
- Helsinki playbook is repeatable;
- second-city activation reaches defined liquidity milestones faster/cheaper than first city;
- operational/support/safety load scales predictably;
- Nordic expansion follows country/legal/source readiness, not investor theatre;
- company operating model has reduced critical founder-only dependencies.

## Fundraising narrative
Raise against the next evidence gap. Do not claim a €1B outcome from architecture quality or Finnish TAM. Show compounding local liquidity, repeat offline behaviour, efficient distribution, defensible attendance/social/organizer graphs, monetizable organizer value and evidence that the city playbook travels.
