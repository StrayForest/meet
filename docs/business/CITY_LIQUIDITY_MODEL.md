# City liquidity model

A city is healthy only when users repeatedly see relevant opportunities with credible social activity. Raw event count and MAU are insufficient.

## Core liquidity metrics
- relevant opportunities per active user for next 7 days;
- socially-active opportunities per active user for next 7 days;
- empty-social-card rate;
- median/p25 attendees or expressed social intent per eligible occurrence;
- time to first relevant event view, first intent and first successful IRL participation;
- join conversion and join→attendance;
- Pod availability/use where enabled;
- 30/60-day repeat successful IRL participation;
- supply freshness/cancellation correctness;
- organizer response/approval latency where applicable.

## Initial Helsinki targets
Starting experiment targets, to be calibrated with evidence:
- >=80% of activated users see >=10 relevant upcoming physical opportunities within configured radius/7 days;
- >=60% see >=3 opportunities with meaningful Meet social activity;
- empty-social-card rate trends downward as activation grows;
- median time-to-first-social-intent <7 days for activated users;
- H2–H4 targets from `PMF_HYPOTHESES.md` are evaluated by acquisition/language/category cohort.

## Density before breadth
If Helsinki has weak social density, do not solve it by opening more cities. Concentrate acquisition and host/organizer seeding in categories/time windows where recurring density can form.

## City activation state
`COVERAGE_ONLY → SEEDING → ACTIVE → HEALTHY`.

- COVERAGE_ONLY: imported/public supply exists; no promise of social density.
- SEEDING: targeted hosts/communities/acquisition; marketplace metrics watched daily/weekly.
- ACTIVE: enough relevant/social supply for public growth.
- HEALTHY: repeat behaviour and endogenous/referral activity meet sustained thresholds.

Status is internal product/ops truth and must not be inferred from population or MAU alone.
