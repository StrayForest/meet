# PRODUCT_SENSE — How Meet should make decisions

## Core promise
**Find something worth doing nearby, understand who is going, and join with confidence.**

## Product is successful when
A user transitions from intention to real attendance with low uncertainty and feels safe enough to repeat it.

## Prioritization order
1. Useful event supply.
2. Join confidence and liquidity.
3. Attendance/show-up.
4. Safety/trust.
5. Repeat participation.
6. Organization supply tools.
7. Monetization.
8. Engagement features only if they improve the above.

## What we are not optimizing
- screen time;
- follower counts;
- viral outrage/content;
- profile browsing;
- swipe addiction;
- vanity likes;
- dating conversion.

## Product questions for every feature
- Does this make discovery, joining, attendance or trust materially better?
- Could this create dating drift or unwanted contact?
- Does it expose location or social information unnecessarily?
- Can the user understand the action without learning our internal vocabulary?
- Does it remain useful in a low-density Finnish city?
- Can an organization use it without making the consumer UX feel commercial?

## Finland launch principles
- Nationwide event utility, city-by-city social liquidity.
- Finnish, English and Russian are first-class launch locales.
- Use plain language and 24-hour/local date conventions.
- Do not assume all users are extroverted, young or comfortable approaching strangers.
- Give users enough participant/trust context to reduce uncertainty without turning the app into people-shopping.

## Experiment ethics
A/B tests may improve ordering, copy and conversion, but cannot hide fees, weaken safety, manipulate cancellation or make privacy less understandable.
