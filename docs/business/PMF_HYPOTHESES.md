# PMF hypotheses — falsifiable business contract

Meet must prove that it changes offline social behaviour, not merely that users browse events.

## North-star outcome
**Successful IRL participation:** a user discovers an occurrence through Meet, joins/commits through Meet where applicable, and attendance is later supported by check-in/host/peer or equivalent reconciled evidence.

## Launch hypotheses
These are starting thresholds for Helsinki experiments, not universal truths. They must be revised from evidence, never softened only to declare success.

| ID | Hypothesis | Initial signal | Failure response |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | New activated users find enough relevant supply | >=80% see >=10 relevant upcoming physical opportunities within their configured radius/7 days | improve supply/category/ranking before adding social features |
| H2 | Discovery converts to intent | >=30% of activated users save/join/express social intent within 7 days | fix relevance, trust, event detail and onboarding |
| H3 | Intent becomes attendance | >=50% of Meet joiners show credible attendance evidence | fix reminders, commitment UX, event quality and no-show friction |
| H4 | Attendance creates repeat value | >=25% of first-time attendees complete another successful IRL participation within 30 days | fix social outcomes, follow-up, density and cohort targeting |
| H5 | Social layer adds value beyond an event directory | events with healthy social proof/Pods materially outperform comparable low-social events on join and repeat | simplify/remove weak social mechanics if no causal value emerges |

## Cohorts
Always segment by city, acquisition channel, language (`fi`, `en`, `ru`), newcomer status where voluntarily supplied/derived safely, event category and first-use intent. Never hide weak cohorts inside aggregate averages.

## Kill / pivot criteria
Do not compensate for persistent H2–H4 failure by adding feature breadth. After multiple controlled iterations with adequate city liquidity, persistent weak join, attendance or repeat behaviour triggers product-positioning/cohort/market review before major roadmap expansion.

## Evidence discipline
Dashboard metrics are not sufficient alone. Pair quantitative cohorts with interviews of: users who attended/repeated; users who joined but no-showed; users who browsed but did nothing; organizers whose events produced strong/weak social outcomes.
