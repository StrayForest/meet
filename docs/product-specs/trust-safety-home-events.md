# Product spec — Trust, safety and private-home events

Detailed engineering/security source: `../05_TRUST_SECURITY_PRIVACY.md`.

## Home event gate
Host >=18, phone verified, strong identity verified, participant cap and safety policy pass.

## Address
Public discovery receives coarse area only. Exact address is separately encrypted and disclosed only to eligible confirmed/approved participants within configured policy/window. Generic serializers never include it.

## Safety experience
Before join, explain private-home context and host verification. Offer Share My Plans. Report/block/remove is easy. Avoid fear-heavy UI for normal verified state.

## Moderation
Reports create reason-coded cases/actions/appeals. AI triages; high-impact irreversible enforcement supports human review.

## Anti-dating
Explicit sexual solicitation, dating-ad events and repeated unwanted romantic/sexual outreach violate policy.

## Acceptance
Dedicated exact-location leak tests; staff access audited where appropriate; kill switch can disable home publishing without deploy; pentest before broad rollout.
