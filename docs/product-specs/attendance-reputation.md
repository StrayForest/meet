# Product spec — Attendance, feedback and reputation

## Goal
Measure real participation and create trust without a public social-credit score.

## Attendance signals
QR/code check-in, organizer confirmation, peer confirmation, optional coarse geofence assist. No continuous location tracking.

## Post-event
Did you attend? Who did you meet? Structured signals: respectful, friendly, on time, good organizer, would meet again; private negative/safety feedback; mutual connect.

## Public reputation
Verification badges, attended/hosted counts or bands, reliable participant/host badges and positive aggregates after minimum sample thresholds.

Never: public star rating, public free-form negative review, raw no-show percentage for social punishment.

## Integrity
Prevent obvious reciprocal/self/gaming abuse. Moderation can suppress fraudulent feedback. Derived projections are rebuildable from authoritative records.

## Acceptance
Attendance attribution measurable for North Star; privacy thresholds; disputed/moderated feedback path; accessible post-event flow.
